How this works
During OFF hours this app removes Discord notifications as they arrive. During ON hours it leaves them alone. It cannot flip Discord's own notification switch (Android doesn't allow that), so keep Discord's notifications allowed in Settings. If they're off there, nothing will show at the ON time.
